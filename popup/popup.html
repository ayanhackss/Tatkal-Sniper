Quick Start Guide
  1. Open this popup and enter your IRCTC Username & Password under Login.
  2. Fill in Journey details (From/To, Date, Class & Quota) and your Passengers list.
  3. Click Save Details to save securely on your local browser.
  4. Click Start Auto-Booking. The extension helper console will appear at the bottom of the IRCTC website to guide and auto-fill your forms.
Essential Tatkal Timings
  • AC Classes (3A, 2A, 1A, 3E, CC): Booking opens daily at 10:00 AM.
  • Non-AC Classes (SL, 2S): Booking opens daily at 11:00 AM.
  • Log in exactly 1 to 2 minutes before (09:58 AM or 10:58 AM) to avoid session timeout.
Tatkal Speed Hacks
  • ⚡ Auto-Focus Captcha: Instantly type the Captcha when focused and press Enter to submit. No mouse clicks needed!
  • 💳 Sub-Second Payment: Choose BHIM/UPI as the preferred payment method. Open your UPI app on your phone beforehand to approve the request instantly.
  • 👥 Limit Passengers: Book for 1-2 passengers at a time. Injecting details for larger groups takes slightly longer.
  • 🛡️ Insurance Skip: Ensure Travel Insurance is pre-configured to automatically select Yes/No and bypass the check.
